## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an academic yet warmly approachable environment tailored for higher education in probability and statistics. It departs completely from cold, sterile dashboard aesthetics and stark black-and-white polarities, establishing an inviting, tactile editorial atmosphere inspired by high-end mathematical journals, warm vellum parchment, and contemporary gamified learning platforms.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Designed for university students, self-taught data practitioners, and researchers navigating complex conceptual models. The experience is calibrated to reduce cognitive fatigue, soothe math-induced friction, and project academic rigour alongside encouraging pedagogical feedback.

### Aesthetic Movement
The visual language merges **Modern Editorial Print** with **Refined Tactile Product Design**. It balances structured modularity with warm paper tones, rhythmic margins, and vibrant interactive moments reminiscent of tactile achievement tokens.

## Colors

The palette executes a rigorous 60-30-10 chromatic distribution to maintain visual calmness across long problem-solving and proof-reading sessions:

### 60% Dominant (Canvas & Surfaces)
- **Base Canvas (`#FBF5EC`):** A soft, warm ivory tone that eliminates the harsh glare of standard displays, evoking high-grade archival book paper.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure crisp white reserved for functional containers, problem modules, calculation pads, and interactive cards to introduce natural hierarchy over the ivory background.

### 30% Structural & Content (Deep Wine & Crimson Burgundy)
- **Primary Structural (`#8A1428`):** Deep, velvety burgundy used for primary titles, deep mathematical typography (LaTeX expressions), structural strokes, and navigational frames.
- **Secondary Structural (`#AC0B32`):** Medium burgundy for secondary headers, sublabels, inactive input borders, and formula annotation callouts.
- **Muted Structural Tint (`#8A14281A` / 10% opacity):** Applied to grid dividing rules, subtle inset borders, and math bracket guides. Pure black (`#000000`) is strictly prohibited throughout the entire interface.

### 10% Focal Accents (Active Kinetic Highlights)
- **Primary Accent (`#FF051E`):** Pure crimson, reserved strictly for focal actions: primary submission CTAs, "Check Solution" triggers, and critical state indicators.
- **Supportive Gamification Accent (`#FF836C`):** Warm coral used to soften progress streaks, mastery badges, active step bullets, and celebratory micro-interactions without causing visual tension.

## Typography

Space Grotesk anchors the system with an authentic technical voice. Its idiosyncratic proportions, balanced x-height, and precise mathematical symbols bridge traditional technical literature with modern educational interfaces.

### Application Rules
- **Formulas and LaTeX Blocks:** Rendered using the primary structural color (`#8A1428`) within `body-lg` or dedicated computational blocks. Mathematical notations receive relaxed tracking to preserve character spacing.
- **Hierarchical Contrast:** Titles leverage tighter negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic crafted editorial titling, while micro-labels and step counters employ open tracking (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) to ensure legibility across small cards.
- **Readability & Weight Distribution:** Dense descriptive theorem text stays on `body-md` (Regular 400). Interactive labels and formula premises use Medium (500) and SemiBold (600) to keep structural hierarchy clear without introducing heavy weights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid for desktop environments (scaling to a 4-column system on mobile devices), emphasizing focused, reading-first single-column streams for calculations and modular two-column views for problem-and-graph workspaces.

### Rhythm Principles
- **Adaptive Canvas:** Desktop screens clamp main exercise flows to a maximum reading width of 840px, centering mathematical derivations while keeping interactive probability sandbox controls within immediate reach.
- **Mathematical Step Pacing:** Vertical gaps between consecutive algebraic derivations adhere strictly to `space-md`, while thematic breaks between problem statements, calculation blocks, and solution validation use `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids dark, heavy synthetic drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Paper Stacking** and **Warm Ambient Illumination**. Depth communicates interactive readiness rather than physical distance.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Base Canvas:** `#FBF5EC` acts as the zero-elevation backdrop.
- **Tonal Layer 1 (Surface Cards):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` containers resting over `#FBF5EC`, framed with an ultra-fine, translucent structural outline (`1px solid rgba(138, 20, 40, 0.12)`). No shadow is required here; the contrast between warm cream and white produces crisp, quiet separation.
- **Tonal Layer 2 (Floating Modals & Interactive Steps):** Elevated `#FFFFFF` surfaces rely on a warm-tinted shadow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(138, 20, 40, 0.08)`), introducing a subtle crimson-undertone ambient glow instead of a sterile gray drop shadow.
- **Gamified Feedback Layer:** Solution check bars and interactive tokens adopt subtle bottom borders (2px to 3px solid `#8A142826`) to introduce a friendly, tactile push-button quality without resorting to skeuomorphic clunkiness.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius) to introduce a soft, human feel that reduces the perceived intimidation of mathematical statistics.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Input Fields & Small Cells:** Styled with `0.5rem` (`rounded`) corners, creating stable, clean rectangular rhythm across matrix tables, probability formulas, and continuous number inputs.
- **Cards & Working Panels:** Use `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) to visually frame problem sets and interactive distribution graphs with approachable warmth.
- **Pills & Achievement Badges:** Streak badges, module tags, and completion meters take fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to maintain a lively gamified feel within the academic framework.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Comprobar Respuesta" / "Continuar"):** Solid `#FF051E` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.75rem` vertical padding, with a subtly darker baseline border (`#D00016`) for tactile feedback. Hover transitions into `#E00017`.
- **Secondary (Step Reveal / Hint):** Transparent base with a 1.5px structural border in `#8A1428`, `#8A1428` text, transitioning to `#8A14280F` background tint on hover.
- **Ghost:** `#8A1428` text with zero border; activates with a warm background wash (`#FBF5EC`) on press.

### Cards & Problem Containers
- Built on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle `1px solid rgba(138, 20, 40, 0.12)` borders.
- Header titles are set in `headline-sm` with `#8A1428`. Subtext and contextual problem assumptions stay in `body-md` using `#8A1428` at 85% opacity.

### Interactive Form Inputs & Numeric Step Fields
- **Default State:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px solid rgba(138, 20, 40, 0.2)` border, and `#8A1428` text.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#AC0B32`, supported by a soft halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(172, 11, 50, 0.12)`).
- **Correct Submission:** Border shifts to an organic sage emerald, paired with a celebratory micro-badge in `#FF836C`.
- **Incorrect Submission:** Border shifts to `#FF051E`, opening an immediate ivory-tinted explanation drawer below.

### Chips & Variable Selectors
- Compact interactive selectors for statistical variables (e.g., $\mu$, $\sigma^2$, $p$-value).
- **Unselected:** Warm ivory background (`#FBF5EC`), border `1px solid rgba(138, 20, 40, 0.15)`, `#8A1428` text.
- **Selected:** `#8A1428` background, `#FFFFFF` text, accompanied by smooth scale feedback.

### Progress Meters & Gamified Elements
- **Daily Streak & Mastery Badges:** Rendered with warm coral accents (`#FF836C`) and clean ivory backing, highlighting numerical achievements without disrupting the academic tone.
- **Progress Track:** Subdued ivory-burgundy track (`rgba(138, 20, 40, 0.08)`) filled with a vibrant `#FF051E` indicator bar, rounded on both ends.

### Formulas & Math Blocks
- Standalone equation displays sit on an elevated `#FFFFFF` card, framed by a soft vertical accent line (3px solid `#AC0B32`) along the left edge.
- Mathematical notation is rendered in `#8A1428`, maintaining clear contrast against the pure white surface.
